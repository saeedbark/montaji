Step-by-step user guide

Everything you need to make a video from your photos and clips: adding files, arranging and trimming, music, text, effects and overlays, auto captions, the AI assistant, then saving the project and downloading the video in high quality.

1Starting the app

  1. Once only: install Node.js (the LTS version) from nodejs.org like any other program.
  2. Open the app folder and double-click the start file: start.bat on Windows, start.command on Mac, or start.sh on Linux.
  3. A small window appears saying to keep it open, then the editor opens in your browser by itself at http://localhost:4747.
  4. When you are done, save your project, then close the browser tab and the small window.
!

Which browser: use a recent Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge; export relies on features that not every browser has.

✓

Your files stay private: editing and export run on your device, and your photos and clips are never uploaded. The only exception is what you ask the AI assistant or auto captions to do: they send what they need to the provider whose key you chose.

✓

Language: the EN button in the top bar switches the whole editor to English, and ع switches it back to Arabic, without losing anything in your project.

2Quick start

Five steps are enough for your first video:

  1. Add your files: press Media and pick your photos and clips. They are added to the timeline in order.
  2. Arrange them: drag any clip left or right to move it, and drag its edge to shorten it.
  3. Add music: press Music and pick an audio file, then Sync cuts to the beat if you want the clips to change with the music.
  4. Add text: press Text and type the title, then drag it in the preview to where you want it.
  5. Export: press Export, choose the resolution, then Download video.
✓

Try it first: press “Try a sample” on the start screen to create a small project with photos, music and text, so you can learn the tools before using your own files.

✓

Or just ask: open the Assistant, attach your files and write, for example, “make a vertical TikTok video from these photos with a title and transitions”. See The AI assistant.

  • 2Preview: shows the video as it will come out, with the play button, the time and a camera button that saves the current frame as an image, and a full-screen button that enlarges the preview (or press F; Esc to leave).

Moving through time

  • Click the ruler at the top of the timeline, or drag along it, to move the playhead to any moment.
  • The Zoom slider zooms the timeline in for precise work or out to see the whole project.
  • The four-arrow button next to the zoom fits the whole project on screen.
  • Drag the top edge of the timeline up or down to make it taller or shorter; double-click it to reset the height.

On phones and small screens

The preview and the timeline stay visible together, and the properties become a bottom drawer opened by the Properties button or by tapping the selected item again. As the window gets narrower, the top bar buttons shrink to icons.

4Adding photos and clips

There are two ways:

  • Press Media and pick one file or several at once.
  • Or drag files from your folder and drop them anywhere on the page.

Each new file is added at the end of the clips track. A photo shows for 4 seconds by default, a video for its full length. To put a picture on top of the video (a logo, say) use Overlay instead.

TypeAccepted formats
PhotosJPG, PNG, WebP, GIF, BMP, AVIF
VideoMP4, WebM, MOV, M4V, MKV — MP4 is the safest
AudioMP3, WAV, M4A, AAC, OGG, FLAC
Project.montaji — opens with everything in it (see Saving projects)
!

iPhone photos in HEIC and some MOV clips may not open in the browser. Convert them to JPG or MP4 first.

5Arranging, trimming and splitting

Reordering

Drag a clip from its middle to its new place. A pink line shows where it will land.

Trimming

Click the clip, then drag one of its two white edges inward. For a photo this changes how long it shows.

Splitting

Put the playhead at the cut point and press Split; the clip becomes two independent parts.

Duplicate and delete

Select the item, then press Duplicate to copy it or Delete to remove it.

These buttons work on every kind of item: clips, texts, overlays, effects, music and caption blocks.

Removing a part from the middle of a clip

  1. Put the playhead at the start of the unwanted part and press Split.
  2. Put the playhead at its end and press Split again.
  3. Click the middle part, then Delete. The remaining parts close the gap by themselves.
✓

Made a mistake? Press the undo arrow in the top bar or Ctrl + Z to go back a step, and repeat to go back further. This also covers what the AI assistant did.

Clips always sit side by side with no gaps. The final video length = the sum of the clip lengths, unless an overlay runs past them, in which case the video runs on with it.

6Music and the beat

  1. Press Music and pick the audio file. It appears as a green bar in the music track, with small marks for the beats.
  2. Drag the bar to choose when the music starts, and drag its edges to pick the part you want.
  3. Click the bar to edit its properties in the side panel.
PropertyWhat it does
VolumeHow loud the music is. Lower it if your clips have speech you want to hear. It can be animated with keyframes (dip under speech, then come back up).
Fade inThe music starts quiet and rises over the chosen time.
Fade outThe music lowers gently before its end instead of stopping abruptly.

Sync cuts to the beat

The music properties have a Beat section with its tempo (BPM). Choose how many beats per clip (Every beat, Every 2 beats, Every 4 beats or Strong beats only), then press Sync cuts to the beat: each clip is timed so that every cut lands on a beat. Run it again after any change to the order or number of clips. The “Pulse on the music beat” option in a clip's properties makes the picture pulse on every beat.

  • If the music is longer than the video, it is cut at the end automatically with a fade out.
  • If it is shorter, select it and press Duplicate to add a copy right after it.
  • You can add more than one audio file, such as music and a voice-over together.
  • To mute or lower a video clip's own sound, click the clip and use “Clip volume” or “Mute clip”.
  • The Extract the sound button in a video clip's properties moves its sound to the music track, in sync (so you can edit it on its own), or downloads it as a file.

7Adding text

  1. Move the playhead to the moment the text should appear.
  2. Press Text and type in the text box of the side panel. Press Enter for a new line.
  3. Drag the text in the preview to place it, or choose “Top / Middle / Bottom”.
  4. On the timeline: drag the yellow bar to change when it appears, and drag its edge to change how long.
PropertyWhat it does
Size, font and colorThe basic look of the text, with a bold option, and an “Elegant italic” font for Latin words in product ads.
OutlineA border around the letters that keeps the text readable over any picture.
Text backgroundA semi-transparent colored box behind the text, good for captions.
ShadowA soft shadow that lifts the text off the background.
Position, opacity and rotationFine control with sliders; each one can be animated with keyframes.
Entrance animationNone, fade, rise from below, or a zoom pop.

To turn speech in your video into text automatically, use Auto captions instead of typing it.

8Transitions and clip look

Click any photo or clip on the timeline to see its options in the properties panel.

Entry transition

How the clip comes in after the one before it: Fade, Slide, Zoom, Wipe or Dip to black, with a “Transition length” slider. A clip with a transition shows a ⇄ mark.

To use the same transition across the whole video, press “Apply this transition to all clips”.

Camera motion

Brings still photos to life: a slow Zoom in or Zoom out, a pan to the Right or Left, Up and Down (travels the full length of a tall image), or Handheld, which makes the photo look filmed by hand.

Fit

Used when the photo's shape differs from the video size, such as a portrait photo in a landscape video:

OptionResult
Blurred backgroundThe whole photo shows, and the sides are filled with a blurred copy of it. (Default)
Black barsThe whole photo shows with black bars at the sides.
Fill the frameThe photo is enlarged to cover the whole screen and the extra is cropped.

Filters and color

Pick a ready filter (Vivid, Warm, Cool, Black and white, Vintage, Soft), then adjust Brightness, Contrast and Saturation with the sliders if you like.

Zoom and position

Zoom, Horizontal and Vertical offset and Opacity sliders to frame the picture exactly; all of them can be animated with keyframes. The “Turn into an overlay” button moves the clip to the overlays track.

Video speed

Video clips only: 0.5× for slow motion, or 1.5× and 2× to speed up. The clip's length changes to match.

Editing a photo

Photos in the project have an “Edit image: crop, remove background…” button that opens them in the Image studio; the changes apply to the photo in the project right away.

9The effects track

Effects sit on their own track over the picture and change everything under them for as long as they last. Press Effect to add one at the playhead, choose its type, intensity and duration in the panel, and drag it on the timeline like any item.

GroupEffects
On the cut (short bursts)Zoom blur, Whip, Flash, Shake, Color split
Over a stretch of timeBlack and white, Sharpen, Vignette, Darken
On the productZoom to product, Spotlight, Shine, Glow, Sparkle
On part of the pictureArea flash, Backlight (a stand, a price tag, a counter)
  • Put it on every cut: a button in the effect's properties that copies it onto every cut between two clips. These copies are linked to the cut and follow it when the timing changes (after a beat sync, for example); “Unlink and pin it in place” frees one.
  • Product effects: select the effect, then drag the circle in the preview over the product, or set its position and size in the panel.
  • Area flash and Backlight: drag the rectangle in the preview over the part that flashes, and set its width and height in the panel.

10Overlays

An overlay is a photo or video drawn on top of the main track: a logo in the corner, picture-in-picture, a product with its background removed… It can start at any time you choose.

  1. Press Overlay and pick a photo or video. It appears in the “Overlays” track at the playhead.
  2. In the preview: drag it to move it, drag a corner to resize it, and the top handle to rotate it (with the mouse or by touch).
  3. In the panel: opacity, Mask (rounded corners or circle), border and its color, shadow, Blend mode, in and out animation, and filter.
  • Order: each overlay has its own row, and the higher one is drawn on top. “Bring forward” and “Send backward” change that.
  • Video overlays play in the preview and their sound is mixed into the export; they have speed, volume and mute like clips.
  • “Move to the main track” turns an overlay back into a normal clip, and “Turn into an overlay” in a clip's properties does the opposite.
✓

A logo without a background: remove the image background first in the Image studio and save it as PNG, then add it as an overlay.

11Keyframes (animation)

Keyframes make any property change over time: text moving across the screen, an overlay growing, music dipping under speech.

  1. Select the item and put the playhead where the motion starts.
  2. Press ◇ next to the property (position, size, zoom, opacity, rotation, volume…); its value is pinned here and the mark turns to ◆.
  3. Move the playhead somewhere else and change the value; a second keyframe is added by itself, and the property animates between the two.
  • Keyframes show as dots on the item in the timeline: click one to move the playhead to it, drag it to change its time, or click it then press Delete to remove it.
  • The “Keyframes” section of the panel has Previous and Next keyframe, the motion to the next keyframe (Smooth, Linear or Hold), and deleting the keyframes at one point or all of them.

12Auto captions

Turns the speech in your video into synced text at the bottom of the screen, highlighting the word being spoken.

  1. From Tools choose Auto captions.
  2. Choose the source (the whole project sound, or the selected video or audio) and the language, or leave it on “Automatic”.
  3. After transcription the blocks appear in a new “Captions” track. Click any block to fix its text, split it or merge it with the next one, and drag its edges to adjust the timing.
  4. Under “Caption style”, pick the font, size, position, colors and background for all blocks at once, and the Spoken word highlight: colored word, enlarged word, or word by word.

Captions are burned into the video on export. You can also export them as a separate SRT or VTT file (from a block's properties or from “Project → Export captions”) for YouTube and video players.

!

Needs a key: transcription runs through a speech-to-text service with your key: Groq (free and good with Arabic), OpenAI or Gemini. Add a Groq key from the settings button in the assistant panel. The audio is sent to that service; for full privacy you can run Whisper on your own computer (see .env.example).

13The AI assistant

Write what you want in your own words, and the assistant does it on the timeline right away: ordering, trimming, text, transitions, effects, beat sync, changing the size…

Turning it on the first time

  1. Press Assistant in the top bar to open its panel.
  2. Choose the provider and paste your key, then Save and enable. Google Gemini gives a key for free; OpenAI, Claude, OpenRouter and others work too.
  3. The key is saved in the .env file in the app folder, on your device only. To change it later, press the settings button in the panel header.

Using it

  • Attach your photos, clips and music with the paperclip button or by drag and drop, then write your request and press Enter.
  • Before its first big change the assistant may ask a few short questions with ready answers (where you will post, which music, which words on screen, calm or lively). Answer in a word, or say “you decide”.
  • The stop button halts it at any moment, and Ctrl + Z undoes what it did.
  • If you have more than one provider, choose the model from the list at the top of the panel.
  • New chat keeps the previous one, and the clock button lists previous chats to go back to.
  • Drag the inner edge of the panel to make it wider or narrower.

Ask about one item

When you select any item (clip, overlay, text, effect, music, caption) a small ✦ button appears on it in the timeline, and an Ask AI button at the top of its properties. Both open the assistant focused on that item alone, with ready suggestions for its type. Write, say, “make it slower and add a zoom” and it applies to that item only. The × on the card above the text box ends the focus.

Remix: “the same as this video, with my photos”

Attach a video you like (a reel, an ad or a template) together with your photos and clips, and write “remix” or “the same as this video with my photos”. The assistant analyzes the reference's cuts, shot lengths, transitions, camera zooms, flashes and music beat, then builds the same edit with your files.

✓

For developers: the editor runs an MCP server at http://localhost:4747/mcp, so an agent such as Claude Code can edit the open project. Its requests show up in a chat of their own in the panel. Details are in the README.

14The Tools menu

The Tools button in the top bar groups standalone tools that run on your device:

Auto captions

Turn the speech in a video into synced text. See Auto captions.

Image studio

Crop, rotate, flip and resize, filters and color, remove the background (automatically or by clicking the color), and convert between PNG, JPEG and WebP. Open several images and download them together as a ZIP.

Merge images

Combine two or more images into one: side by side, stacked, or a grid, with spacing, border, rounded corners and background color.

Video to audio

Extract the sound of any video, pick a part of it on its waveform, then download it as M4A or WAV or add it to the project.

Split video

Cut a video into separate MP4 files: every set length (30 seconds for WhatsApp stories, say), equal parts, or at times you choose. “Exact cut” places each cut exactly where you asked.

Save a frame as an image

The camera button under the preview saves the current moment of the video as an image on your device.

16Saving and opening projects

Save your work to finish it later or move it to another computer:

  • Save: the Save button in the top bar (next to undo), or the Project menu → Save project, or Ctrl + S. This downloads one .montaji file with the timeline and all the media, including overlays, captions and keyframes. In Chrome and Edge you choose where the file goes on the first save only; after that it saves straight into the same file. A project you open from the Open project window also saves straight back into its file. For a copy elsewhere use Save as… (Ctrl + Shift + S).
  • Open: Project menu → Open project, or Ctrl + O, or drag the file onto the editor, or “Open a saved project” on the start screen.
!

Save before closing: the editor does not save by itself. If you close or reload the page without saving, your work is lost (the browser warns you first).

17Export and download

  1. Press Export in the top bar.
  2. Choose the Resolution, Frame rate and Quality. The approximate file size shows below them.
  3. Press Start export and wait for the progress bar to finish. Keep the page open.
  4. Watch the result in the preview window, then press Download video to save an MP4 file to your Downloads folder.
ResolutionWhen to choose it
HD — 720pQuick sharing over WhatsApp; small file and faster export.
Full HD — 1080pThe best fit for most uses and social media. (Default)
2K — 1440pSharper for big screens.
4K — 2160pThe highest resolution. Takes longer, needs a strong computer, and the file is large.
  • Frame rate: 30 always works. Choose 60 for smoother motion if your clips were filmed at 60, or 24 for a cinematic look.
  • Quality: “High” is a good balance. “Ultra” gives the sharpest result at a larger size, “Normal” a smaller file, and “Compact” a very small file for sharing (about a quarter of “High”).
  • Set a size: pick it from the Quality list and type the size you want in megabytes, for example 35 for a 3 minute video, and the file comes out close to it. If the size is small for the chosen resolution, the dialog suggests a lower one where the picture is sharper, with a button to switch to it.
  • Slow computer: 720p at 24 fps exports much faster than 1080p at 30.
!

Export time: it can take several times the length of the video, and more at higher resolutions and with long video clips. You can stop it at any moment with “Stop export”.

✓

An exported video file cannot be edited afterwards. Save the project too if you may need to change something later.

19Troubleshooting

The page does not open after starting the app

Make sure the small window is still open, then type http://localhost:4747 in the address bar. If start.bat opened the Node.js download page, Node.js is not installed: install it, then run the file again.

A “could not open the file” message appears

The browser does not support that format. Convert photos to JPG and videos to MP4, then try again.

The assistant asks for a key or rejects it

Make sure you copied the whole key with no spaces, and that it is from the same provider you chose in the list. If the assistant says the local server is not running, start the app again from the start file.

Auto captions do not work

They need a Groq, OpenAI or Gemini key (add it from the settings button in the assistant panel), and the project needs audible speech: a video whose sound is not muted, or a voice recording.

“Your browser does not support export”

Open the app in Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge updated to the latest version.

“This resolution is not supported on your device”

Your computer cannot encode that resolution. Choose a lower one such as 1080p.

Export is very slow

Choose a lower resolution or 30 frames instead of 60, close other programs and tabs, and keep the editor window visible until it finishes.

The exported video has no sound

Check that the music “Volume” is not zero, that “Mute clip” is off, and that the music sits within the video's length, not after its end.

The music stops before the video ends

The audio file is shorter than the video. Select the music bar and press “Duplicate”, add another audio file after it, or pick fewer beats per clip when syncing.

The preview stutters while playing

This happens with heavy clips or many overlays on mid-range computers and does not affect the final file; export processes every frame on its own.
