1Starting the app

  1. Open the app folder and double-click start.bat (Windows) or start.command (Mac).
  2. A small terminal window appears (leave it open), then the editor opens in your browser at http://localhost:4747.
  3. When you are done, close the browser tab and then the terminal window.
!

Which browser: use a recent Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge; export relies on technology not every browser has.

✓

Your privacy is safe: everything runs on your device. Your photos and clips are never uploaded.

✓

Language: the EN / ع button in the top bar switches the editor between English and Arabic. Your choice is remembered.

2Quick start

Five steps are enough for your first video:

  1. Add your files: press Media and choose your photos and clips. They are added to the timeline in order.
  2. Arrange them: drag any clip left or right to move it, and drag its edge to shorten it.
  3. Add music: press Music and choose an audio file.
  4. Add text: press Text and type your title, then drag it in the preview to where you want it.
  5. Export: press Export video, choose the resolution, then Download video.
✓

Try it first: press “Try a sample” on the start screen to create a small project with photos, music and text, so you can learn the tools before using your own files.

4Adding photos and clips

There are two ways:

  • Press Media and choose one or several files.
  • Or drag files from your folder and drop them anywhere on the page.

Each new file is added at the end of the clips track. A photo shows for 4 seconds by default, a video for its full length.

TypeAccepted formats
PhotosJPG, PNG, WebP, GIF, BMP, AVIF
VideoMP4, WebM, MOV, M4V, MKV — MP4 is the safest
AudioMP3, WAV, M4A, AAC, OGG, FLAC
!

iPhone photos in HEIC format and some MOV clips may not open in the browser. Convert them to JPG or MP4 first.

5Arranging, trimming and splitting

Reorder

Drag a clip from its middle to its new place. A pink line shows where it will land.

Trim

Click the clip, then drag one of its two white edges inwards. For a photo this changes how long it shows.

Split

Put the playhead at the cut point and press Split: the clip becomes two independent parts.

Duplicate and delete

Select the item, then press Duplicate to copy it or Delete to remove it.

Removing a part from the middle of a clip

  1. Put the playhead at the start of the unwanted part and press Split.
  2. Put the playhead at its end and press Split again.
  3. Click the middle part, then Delete. The remaining parts close the gap automatically.
✓

Made a mistake? Press the undo arrow in the top bar or Ctrl + Z to go back a step, and repeat to go back further.

Clips always sit next to each other with no gaps. The final video length = the sum of the clip lengths on this track.

6Adding music

  1. Press Music and choose the audio file. It appears as a green bar on the music track.
  2. Drag the bar to choose when the music starts, and drag its edges to pick the part you want.
  3. Click the bar to edit its properties in the side panel.
PropertyWhat it does
VolumeHow loud the music is. Lower it if your clips have speech you want to hear.
Fade inThe music starts quiet and rises over the set time.
Fade outThe music fades gently before its end instead of stopping abruptly.
  • If the music is longer than the video it is cut at the end automatically, with a fade out.
  • If it is shorter, select it and press Duplicate to add a copy right after it.
  • You can add more than one audio file, such as music and a voice-over together.
  • To mute or lower a video clip’s own sound: click the clip, then use “Clip volume” or “Mute clip”.

7Adding text

  1. Move the playhead to the moment the text should appear.
  2. Press Text and type in the box in the side panel. Press Enter for a new line.
  3. Drag the text in the preview to place it, or choose “Top / Middle / Bottom”.
  4. On the timeline: drag the yellow bar to change when it appears, and drag its edge to change how long it stays.
PropertyWhat it does
Size, font and colorThe basic look of the text, with a bold option.
OutlineA border around the letters that keeps the text readable over any picture.
Text backgroundA semi-transparent colored box behind the text, good for captions.
ShadowA soft shadow that lifts the text off the background.
Entrance animationNone, fade, rise from below, or a pop.

8Transitions and effects

Click any photo or clip on the timeline to see its options in the properties panel.

Entry transition

How a clip appears after the one before it: Fade, Slide, Zoom, Wipe or Dip to black. A clip with a transition shows a ⇄ mark.

To use the same transition across the whole video, press “Apply this transition to all clips”.

Camera motion

Brings still photos to life: a slow zoom in or zoom out, or a pan to the right or left.

Fit

Used when the photo’s shape differs from the video size, such as a portrait photo in a landscape video:

OptionResult
Blurred backgroundThe whole photo shows, and the sides are filled with a blurred copy of it. (Default)
Black barsThe whole photo shows with black bars on the sides.
Fill the frameThe photo is enlarged to cover the whole screen and the excess is cropped.

Filters and color

Pick a ready filter (Vivid, Warm, Cool, Black and white, Vintage, Soft), then adjust Brightness, Contrast and Saturation with the sliders if you like.

Video speed

For video clips only: 0.5× for slow motion, or 1.5× and 2× to speed up. The clip’s length changes accordingly.

10Export and download

  1. Press Export video in the top bar.
  2. Choose the Resolution, Frame rate and Quality. The approximate file size shows below them.
  3. Press Start export and wait for the progress bar to finish. Keep the page open.
  4. Watch the result in the preview window, then press Download video to save an MP4 file to your downloads folder.
ResolutionWhen to choose it
HD — 720pQuick sharing on WhatsApp: a small file and a faster export.
Full HD — 1080pThe best choice for most uses and social media. (Default)
2K — 1440pSharper for large screens.
4K — 2160pThe highest resolution. Takes longer, needs a strong device, and the file is large.
  • Frame rate: 30 always works. Choose 60 for smoother motion if your clips were filmed at 60.
  • Quality: “High” is a good balance. “Ultra” gives the sharpest result at a larger size, and “Normal” a smaller file.
!

Export time: it can take several times the video’s length, more at higher resolutions and with long video clips. You can stop it at any moment with “Stop export”.

!

Save before closing: if you close or reload the page, unsaved work is lost. Save the project (Ctrl + S) or export the video first.

12Troubleshooting

The page does not open after running start.bat

Make sure the terminal window is still open, then type http://localhost:4747 in the address bar. If the window says the node command is not recognized, Node.js is not installed on the computer.

A “Could not open the file” message appears

The browser does not support that format. Convert photos to JPG and videos to MP4, then try again.

“Your browser cannot export”

Open the app in Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge updated to the latest version.

“This resolution is not supported on your device”

Your device cannot encode that resolution. Choose a lower one such as 1080p.

Export is very slow

Choose a lower resolution or 30 frames instead of 60, close other programs and tabs, and keep the editor window visible until it finishes.

The exported video has no sound

Check that the music’s “Volume” is not zero, that “Mute clip” is off, and that the music sits within the video’s length, not after its end.

The music stops before the video ends

The audio file is shorter than the video. Select the music bar and press “Duplicate”, or add another audio file after it.

The preview stutters while playing

This happens with heavy clips on mid-range devices and does not affect the final file; export processes every frame on its own.
